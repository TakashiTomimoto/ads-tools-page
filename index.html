takashi-ads-tools

Internal reporting tool.

This is a personal, internal tool used solely by its developer to read Google Ads reporting and keyword planning data via the Google Ads API, for the developer's own advertising account. It is not distributed to other users and has no public sign-up.

The tool uses read-only reporting calls (campaign performance, keyword ideas) and does not modify ad accounts on behalf of any third party.

Privacy Policy

This tool accesses only the developer's own Google Ads account data via OAuth, for the sole purpose of generating reports for the developer. No data collected through the Google Ads API is shared with, sold to, or processed by any third party. No data is stored outside the developer's own local environment.
